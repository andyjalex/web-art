SandCut

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

SandCut is an iPhone app that finds the points in your beach volleyball videos and cuts them into clips. It is run by Andy Alexander, an individual developer based in Bournemouth, UK, who is the data controller for the personal data described here.

Questions about this policy or your data: email andy@web-art.io.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your videos to train our AI model.

What we collect

Data What it is When
Account ID A random user ID created when you first open the app, before you sign up Always
Email and password Your login, if you create an account. Passwords are handled by Firebase and never seen by us Only if you create an account
Device ID An ID for your phone, used only so free credits are given once per device Always
Videos you upload The video file, a lower-resolution copy, and the clips and timings SandCut produces When you analyse a video
Credits and purchases Your credit balance and a record of each credit pack bought. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details When you buy credits
Technical logs Server logs such as IP address, request times and errors When the app talks to our server

Videos may show other people. Only upload footage you have the right to share.

How we use it

We use your data only to run SandCut:

  • To find the points in your videos and create your clips (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To keep your account, credits and purchases in step across sessions and devices (to provide the service).
  • To stop free credits being claimed more than once per device (our legitimate interest in preventing abuse).
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure, using server logs (our legitimate interest).
  • To keep purchase records for tax and accounting (legal obligation).

Your videos are not used to train or improve SandCut's AI model. If that ever changes, it will only happen with your explicit opt-in.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes.

Provider What for Data
Google Cloud and Firebase Hosting, video processing, storage, login and account database Videos, account ID, email, credits, logs. Videos are stored in London (europe-west2)
RevenueCat Checking in-app purchases with Apple Account ID, purchase records
Apple Taking payment for credit packs Handled under Apple's own privacy policy

Some providers may process data outside the UK, for example in the US. Where they do, they rely on approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

We will only share data otherwise if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Data Kept for
Uploaded videos, copies and clips Deleted automatically 7 days after upload. Save any clips you want to keep before then
Account, email and credit balance Until you delete your account
Device ID Kept so free credits are only given once per device
Purchase records As long as needed for tax and accounting, usually 6 years
Server logs Up to 30 days

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This removes your login and account data straight away. Your videos are removed within 7 days.

Under UK data protection law you can also ask to see, correct, move or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

SandCut is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
